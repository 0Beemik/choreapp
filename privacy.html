Family Chores

Privacy Policy

Last updated October 29, 2026

Family Chores is built for families, including children. We keep it simple: we don't run servers, we don't have accounts, and we don't collect your family's information.

Information stored on your devices

Names, avatars, chores, points, coins and activity you enter stay on your family's own phones and tablets. We never receive them.

Home Wi-Fi sync

If you connect more than one device, they share data directly with each other over your home Wi-Fi network. Nothing is sent over the internet or to us.

Notifications

The app shows notifications on the device (for example, when a chore is traded). These are created on the device and are not sent through any outside service.

Ads

The app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. All ad requests are marked as directed at children and limited to G-rated content, so ads are not personalized and are not based on your behavior. Google may collect limited device information, such as IP address and device identifiers, to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app is designed to be set up by a parent, and nothing a child enters leaves the family's devices except as described in the Ads section above.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, removes all of its data from that device.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.

Contact

Questions? Open an issue on GitHub.
